# v0.5

## MODIFY keyword scheme generalized to N, SLOW/STUN/AMP repointed

`abilityHalfIconTranslator.ts`'s MODIFY keyword pills were fixed-value exact matches (`AMP`,
`STUN`, hardcoded `SLOW`). Generalized to `N`-parameterized regex patterns so any delta renders
correctly, and repointed two keywords to attributes that better match their name:

- `SLOW` moved off `ACTION_COUNT` (which fully blocked actions, functionally a weak `STUN`) onto
  the `speed_increment` attribute added in the PieceMovement migration — `SLOW N` now actually
  shortens movement range instead of draining actions.
- `STUN`'s sentinel `-99` (whole-turn action wipe) rewritten to `-1` across all 6 carriers
  (5 live catalog pieces + the disabled `.turret/stun-turret.json`) — `STUN N` is now a real
  N-scaled action drain, not a fixed full-turn lock.
- `AMP` renamed `SURGE` for symmetry with the new `QUICKEN`/`HASTE`/`ENCUMBER` family.
- Icons2 (`abilityHalfIconTranslator.ts`) MODIFY pills now drop the pencil lead icon entirely
  when a keyword resolves — label-only, pencil reserved for the raw-DSL fallback case.
- `KILL` effect lines render as a `BOMB` pill (icon swapped, not appended) when their target is a
  board-pattern zone — single-target/SELF/DEFENDER kills keep the plain Axe icon.

## New keywords: QUICKEN, HASTE, ENCUMBER, DISCOUNT, INFLATE

Extended the MODIFY keyword vocabulary to attributes that had no keyword coverage yet
(`action_cost`, permanent `summon_cost`, positive `speed_increment`):

- `QUICKEN N` (`SPEED_INCREMENT +N TURNS 1`) — SLOW's positive counterpart.
- `HASTE N` / `ENCUMBER N` (`ACTION_COST ∓N TURNS 1`) — first keywords targeting `action_cost`.
- `DISCOUNT N` / `INFLATE N` (`SUMMON_COST ∓N TURNS 99`) — permanent-duration economy levers,
  precedented by `trap/trapped-king.json`'s existing `SUMMON_COST -1 TURNS 1 → FRIENDLY SHELF ALL`.

## 6 new cards

- **Sprinting Nomad** — `NOMAD`, `ON MOVE 1 → MODIFY ACTION_COST -1 TURNS 1 → SELF` (HASTE, self)
- **Weary Nomad** — `NOMAD`, `ON MOVE 1 → MODIFY ACTION_COST +1 TURNS 1 → ENEMY BOARD:PATTERN:SQUARE:1 ALL` (ENCUMBER, aoe)
- **Caravan Nomad** — `NOMAD`, `ON MOVE 3 → MODIFY ACTION_COST -1 TURNS 1 → FRIENDLY BOARD:PATTERN:SQUARE:1 ALL` (HASTE, aoe)
- **Grandpa Berserker** — `BERSERKER`, `ON KILL 1 → MODIFY ACTION_COST +1 TURNS 1 → SELF` (ENCUMBER, self drawback)
- **Inflate Trap** — `TRAP`, `ON DEATH 1 → MODIFY SUMMON_COST +2 TURNS 99 → ENEMY SHELF 1`
- **Inflate Colossus** — `TRAP`, `ON DEATH 1 → MODIFY SUMMON_COST +1 TURNS 99 → ENEMY SHELF ALL`

None of the 6 are wired into any `default_bags/*.txt` — catalog-only, matching the Colossal Trap
precedent from `balance_audit_2.md` until a bag-inclusion decision is made.

Full SC breakdowns in `balance_audit_3.md`.

## Deferred: MORPH keyword

`CONVERT ARCHETYPE X TURNS 99` was proposed as `MORPH` but held back. No code-level break exists —
`satisfies_filters` (piece.py:165) already honors active `PieceType` conversions for
`WHERE ARCHETYPE:X` filters, and trigger firing isn't archetype-gated. But `v0.4.md`'s house rule
("a piece's archetype implies its ability trigger", and the shared archetype/trigger icon scheme
built on it) breaks thematically: a converted piece's `ability_dsl` trigger line is fixed at
authoring time, so a BERSERKER MORPHed into a VANGUARD keeps firing `ON KILL` forever while
displaying a VANGUARD icon that implies `ON PROMOTION`. Open until decided whether that mismatch
is acceptable flavor or needs `PieceAbility` to become conversion-aware.
